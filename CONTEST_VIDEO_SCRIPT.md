## 🎬 FINAL TIPS FOR FILMING

### Do's:
✅ Speak with confidence and passion
✅ Make eye contact with camera
✅ Show genuine enthusiasm for the project
✅ Use clear, simple language (avoid jargon)
✅ Demonstrate the product (show, don't just tell)
✅ Keep energy high throughout
✅ End with strong call to action

### Don'ts:
❌ Don't read from script (sound natural)
❌ Don't use too much technical jargon
❌ Don't go over 3 minutes
❌ Don't forget to smile
❌ Don't undersell your achievements
❌ Don't forget to mention Solana multiple times

### Technical Quality:
- Good lighting (natural or ring light)
- Clear audio (use external mic if possible)
- Stable camera (tripod or phone mount)
- Clean background (or use platform as backdrop)
- 1080p minimum resolution
- Professional editing (smooth transitions)
