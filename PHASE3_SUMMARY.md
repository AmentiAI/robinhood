- **Backgrounds:** Gradient from #0a0a0f → #14141e → #1a1a24
- **Text:** #FFFFFF (primary), #a8a8b8 (secondary)

### Typography
- **Headings:** Font-black with gradient text
- **Body:** Font-medium with proper hierarchy
- **Labels:** Font-bold for emphasis

### Spacing
